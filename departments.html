Our Teams

Departments

Discover the specialized teams that drive Clair De Loi forward — and find where you belong.

📝

Newsletter Team

Take detailed notes during workshops, webinars, and meetings and write articles that are published on Clair de Loi's Medium account. Transform key discussions and insights into engaging, accessible content for the wider community.

🔬

Researcher Team

Conduct legal and academic research on assigned topics, using reliable sources to support our workshops, events, and projects. Dive deep into legal issues and help create evidence-based, high-quality content for the community.

🎨

Designer Team

Create visual content for Clair de Loi's social media platforms. Design compelling graphics, infographics, and branding materials that communicate our mission and engage our audience across all channels.

🌍

Chapters

Represent Clair De Loi in different countries or cities by building local communities, recruiting members, and organizing activities and events. Bring our global mission to life at the local level and help expand our reach worldwide.
